About the garden

RoseEmerged is Akai and Hoor’s software portfolio. Each planter holds one project, and its rose grows to the completion percentage on its plaque.

Roses grow automatically as you explore. Your tab remembers the ones you’ve seen. Plaques marked “sample” show illustrative development data.
